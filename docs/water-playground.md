# Experimental water

Water, air and earth are experimental CPU effects under the collapsed **Experimental** disclosure; their materials and motion can still look synthetic.

For water, choose a form and record a held **shhh** or a **sploosh** accent. A prepared hand clip supplies the tracking; use your own tracked clip to try hand movements. Water appears in the finished shot. The live webcam preview supports fire and lightning.

This is a stylized composite from a single camera view. Broad sheets can look synthetic, and dark footage provides little reflected-light information. It is not a physical liquid simulation.

The gallery focuses on fire, explosion and lightning. Retained water comparisons and their source attribution are documented in [the media credits](../samples/CREDITS.md).
