Perform it again yourself: below, pick Your hand: open palm, then Use my microphone.

Tap your palm or your fingertips and make the noise: hum to charge an ember, BOOM to throw the fireball, a long fffff from pointed fingers for a flamethrower that whips through the air when you swing. The fire is rendered once and carried on your hand; Perform it again gives it a new rhythm with no new render. A buzzing zzzzz strikes lightning from your fingertips, a long BOOOOM with a punch sets off an explosion, and water and air bend in your open palm: these are made on this computer from your hand and your voice, no GPU (Choose it yourself under Make the shot). Or tap or draw anything in your shot (pf., KA-BOOM, fwoooOOOM, hissss, a snap in the clip, or press and hold) and it is rendered into the scene, timed and sized by your cue.
