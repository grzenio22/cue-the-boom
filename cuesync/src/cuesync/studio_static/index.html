Cue the Boom

Film your hand. Make the noise. Throw fire.

Tap your palm or your fingertips and make the noise: hum to charge an ember, BOOM to throw the fireball, a long fffff from pointed fingers for a flamethrower that whips through the air when you swing. The fire is rendered once and carried on your hand; Perform it again gives it a new rhythm with no new render. Or tap or draw anything in your shot (pf., KA-BOOM, fwoooOOOM, hissss, a snap in the clip, or press and hold) and it is rendered into the scene, timed and sized by your cue.

1. Pick a shot

Your hand, or a thing (a pumpkin, a mug, a toy), filmed with the phone held still, 1-15 s (the shot uses the first 10 s).

Fire, smoke and steam by LTX-2.5 (Lightricks) and the ainvfx-fluid IC-LoRA (AInVFX), on ComfyUI through the Comfy SDK; hands followed by MediaPipe and RTMPose and matted by SAM 2.1. Open weights only.
