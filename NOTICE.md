source media, generated outputs, procedural effects, screenshots and diagrams:

The submission demo's narration was synthesized with
[Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M). Its background music was
generated locally with Meta's
[MusicGen-small](https://huggingface.co/facebook/musicgen-small), then
crossfade-looped for the film. These models were used only to produce the demo's
narration and music; they are not part of the app's effect pipeline. Fire
performance cues use the author's recorded voice and the four credited CC0
voices, distinct from the synthetic narration. The model cards describe the
respective model-weight licences.

Earth's bundled rock material uses **Rock Boulder Dry** by Dimitrios Savva
(photography) and Rico Cilliers (processing), from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), under
[CC0-1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The photographed diffuse, normal and
ambient-occlusion maps are public domain; they are not generated by LTX. These
maps shade newly exposed fracture interiors.
Per-file download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in
`cuesync/src/cuesync/assets/stone/provenance.json`. The runtime uses bundled files
and makes no requests to Poly Haven. Asset discovery was powered by Poly Haven.

Earth's exterior shape and albedo are adapted from **Rock 07** by Jenelle van
Heerden, [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07), under
[CC0-1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). CueSync radially resamples the scanned
model into a closed, compact surface and spherical material maps. This is an
approximation of the source scan, not an unmodified scan or LTX-generated geometry.
The original and adapted asset hashes are recorded in
`cuesync/src/cuesync/assets/stone/rock_scan.json`; the offline adaptation tool is
`tools/bake_stone_scan.py`. Fracturing, movement, lighting and compositing are
implemented in CueSync. The runtime reads the bundled asset without network access.

The studio's display faces are self-hosted in `web/app/fonts/`: **Cinzel** (The
Cinzel Project Authors) and **Shojumaru** (Astigmatic), both under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1, whose texts sit beside them (`OFL-Cinzel.txt`,
`OFL-Shojumaru.txt`). The element seals on the start page are original drawings.
`web/public/hero/reel.mp4` is cut from three of the sample shots in
`samples/made_ahead/bending/`.
